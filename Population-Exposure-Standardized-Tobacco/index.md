[← Back to Main Repository Landing Page](../)

## Dataset Overview
This directory houses the standardized state-level cigar tax rate datasets, codebooks, and replication scripts tracking tax structures across the United States from 2010 to 2024.

* **Associated Publication:** Standardising the measurement of cigar tax rates in the USA, 2010–2024 (*Tobacco Control*, 2026).
* **DOI Link:** [10.1136/tc-2026-060077](https://doi.org/10.1136/tc-2026-060077)
* **PubMed:** [PMID: 42425894](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42425894/) *(Update PMID if needed)*
