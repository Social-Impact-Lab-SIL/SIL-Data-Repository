Population Exposure to Standardized Tobacco Policies

This repository hosts standardized datasets detailing population exposure to various tobacco control policies across the United States by state, county, and time. When using any of this project's data, please reference the correct peer-reviewed publication listed below. Please see the publications for additional details on how the measures are constructed. Where multiple versions of the data exist, we recommend using the most recent version for new projects.

1. E-Cigarette Taxes

E-cigarette tax schemes vary significantly across states and localities, making cross-sectional and longitudinal comparisons challenging. This project provides standardized e-cigarette tax rates at the state and local levels in the United States.

Version 2 (2010–2023)

Download E-Cigarette Tax Data Version 2 (2010-2023) (Excel Workbook)

Data Description:

  • Includes Closed System E-cigarette Taxes by State/County from 2010 to 2023, utilizing a 35% retailer markup and time-invariant tax units.
  • Includes Open System E-cigarette Taxes by State/County from 2010 to 2023, utilizing a 35% retailer markup and time-invariant tax units.

Associated Publication Citation:

Cotti, Chad, Erik Nesson, Michael F. Pesko, and Serena Phillips. "Standardising the measurement of e-cigarette taxes in the USA (2nd edition), 2010–2023." Tobacco Control 35 (2026): 173-178. PubMed Link

Version 1 (2010–2020)

Download E-Cigarette Tax Data Version 1 (2010-2020) (Excel Workbook)

Data Description:

  • Includes E-cigarette Taxes by State/County from 2010 to 2020 using a 35% retailer markup and time-invariant tax units.
  • Includes E-cigarette Taxes by State/County from 2010 to 2020 using a 20% retailer markup and time-invariant tax units.
  • Includes E-cigarette Taxes by State/County from 2010 to 2020 using a 35% retailer markup and time-varying tax units.

Associated Publication Citation:

Cotti, Chad, Erik Nesson, Michael F. Pesko, Serena Phillips, and Nathan Tefft. "Standardising the measurement of e-cigarette taxes in the USA, 2010–2020." Tobacco Control 32, no. e2 (2023): e251-e254. PubMed Link

2. Cigar Taxes

This project provides standardized cigar tax rates at the state and local levels in the United States, addressing cross-jurisdictional variations in tax structures.

Download Cigar Tax Data (Excel Workbook)

Data Description:

  • Provides standardized longitudinal cigar tax rates across US states and local jurisdictions from 2010 to 2024.
  • Standardizes complex ad valorem and specific tax structures into comparable metrics for empirical research.

Associated Publication Citation:

Scoblic, Guthrie, Rachel Y. L. Fung, Abigail S. Friedman, and Michael F. Pesko. "Standardising the measurement of cigar tax rates in the USA, 2010–2024." Tobacco Control (2026): tc-2026-060077. PubMed Link

3. Flavored Tobacco Product Sales Restrictions

This longitudinal dataset describes state and national population coverage and comprehensiveness of flavored tobacco sales from 2010 to 2023 for e-cigarettes, cigarettes, cigars, and smokeless tobacco.

Download Flavored Tobacco Product Sales Restrictions Version 1 (2010-2023) (Excel Workbook)

Data Description:

  • Covers restrictions from 2010 to 2023 across multiple product categories (e-cigarettes, cigarettes, cigars, and smokeless tobacco).
  • Accounts for varying product, flavor, and retailer exemptions when calculating policy comprehensiveness.

4. Vaping and Smoking Indoor Air Laws

This database reports US national- and state-level estimates of population coverage of comprehensive and partial indoor smoking restrictions from 1990 to 2021 for bars, restaurants, and workplaces, and comprehensive indoor vaping restrictions from 2006 to 2021 for the same locations. Estimates were calculated using policy data from the American Nonsmokers' Rights Foundation.

Download Vaping and Smoking Indoor Air Laws Version 1 (2010-2021) (Excel Workbook)

Data Description:

  • Includes national- and state-level estimates of population coverage for comprehensive and partial indoor smoking restrictions (1990–2021) across bars, restaurants, and workplaces.
  • Includes comprehensive indoor vaping restriction coverage (2006–2021) for bars, restaurants, and workplaces.
  • Available in Excel format with a separate tab for each state.

Associated Publication Citation:

Seidenberg, Andrew B., Karl Braganza, Maxwell Chomas, Megan C. Diaz, Abigail S. Friedman, Serena Phillips, and Michael F. Pesko. "Coverage of Indoor Smoking and Vaping Restrictions in the US, 1990-2021." American Journal of Preventive Medicine 67, no. 4 (2024): 494-502. PubMed Link
