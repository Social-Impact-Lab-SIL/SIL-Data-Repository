# Social Impact Lab Data Repository

Welcome to the open data portal for the **Social Impact Lab**. This repository hosts public research data, codebooks, and replication files associated with our empirical studies.

---

## Usage & Terms
Datasets and documentation in this repository are made available under our [LICENSE](LICENSE). 

* **Citation:** If you use data from this repository in your research, please cite the corresponding paper as noted on each dataset's respective documentation page.
* **Contact:** For questions regarding data structures or replication codes, please reach out via the primary study contacts.

---

## Available Datasets

* [Specialty Tobacco Stores](./Specialty-Tobacco/) — Retail locations, spatial distributions, and visitor counts.
* [ECig Tax and Restrictions](./Population-Exposure-Standardized-Tobacco/) — Policy standards and methodological tracking.
