Social Impact Lab Data Repository

Welcome to the open data portal for the Social Impact Lab. This repository hosts public research data, codebooks, and replication files associated with our empirical studies.

Usage & Terms

Datasets and documentation in this repository are made available under our LICENSE.

  • Citation: If you use data from this repository in your research, please cite the corresponding paper as noted on each dataset's respective documentation page. The article citation is provided in an example format and may be rendered in the style required by the publication or other venue. Users must also cite the versioned OpenICPSR repository record as the source of the data.
  • Contact: For questions regarding data structures or replication codes, please reach out via the primary study contacts.

Available Datasets

Population Exposure to Standardized Tobacco Policies

E-cigarette taxes, indoor air laws, flavored tobacco sales restrictions, cigar taxes, by state/county and time.

Hearing healthcare policy data, by state and time.
